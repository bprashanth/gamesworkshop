The icon is the current reading. The color groups a selected history and terrain context.
These are context names, not danger levels or category colors.

The left box on paper and the stage card use exactly the same context function.
The right-hand word names a category observed in the next historical square.
Its ink repeats the matched context; it does not predict the next live color.

The stage mock is paused after square 06 has been revealed/crossed. Only its four
cards are shown; older readings are stored. Voting selects a card in square 07.
The terrain used by the color is already known here. A reveal of hidden square 07
must not silently use its still-hidden slope to assign a new cue color.

Six colors compress selected features, not the entire map history. Different detailed
histories may share a bucket. This is a hand-designed context key, not transformer
attention or a claim about real Mars.
