About this map

Twenty government survey values shape the relief. Lower age-appropriate attendance produces taller ridges. Ridge positions, folds, erosion and routes are designed for the game. This is not geography, dropout risk or an intervention prediction. No past or future data has been invented.

Routes are illustrative alternatives through the generated terrain, not measured household histories. The relief slider exaggerates height, not statistical confidence.
