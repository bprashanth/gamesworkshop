## Checkpoint 2 — Rule flaw found; one-rule iteration validated

Independent analysis proved that lowest-wins + five-card hands allows each player to conserve the cheapest of every unbuilt suit. All void turns have a duplicate or already-built card to discard. The original five-part objective therefore has no suit-order or signal strategy under a simple policy.

- Baseline: 2,500 seeded games, identical outcomes under every order/signal ablation; 2,494 completed, mean 6.54; the six other deals lacked a suit.
- Changed exactly one central rule: **highest card in the called suit installs**. Lowest total remains the only score. This creates a reason to shed expensive cards before calling their suit, aided by cheap public signals.
- Revised: 16.92 mean with strategy/signals, 20.17 without signals, 17.71 random call order, 21.18 random legal play. 477/500 tested deals changed with first suit.
- Kept the original variant available to simulation; reports contain both baselines and explicit incomplete outcomes. No Phase 2 added.
