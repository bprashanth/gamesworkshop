# Household icon study

Read the Household v5 brief and started an isolated `households/` study. Existing collaborative Rover and competitive Easy/Medium sources and servers remain unchanged. Hard gameplay has not been implemented.

Cursor CLI produced initial SVG families and alternatives. Reviewed actual browser screenshots, corrected alternative-state mappings, enlarged the school doorway/child symbols and simplified the weak crop and low-cash drawings. The primary doorway is more legible than the book alternative. Existing Rover sun and storm are retained; storm is not an inverted kill card in this story.

The review page supports dark/light and primary/alternate icons. The white paper page preserves the familiar left icon/color and right answer layout with ASCII arrows. Its 12 answers are explicitly illustrative, not generated evidence. Screenshots stay local in ignored `households/out/`.

Playwright checked 12 primary cards, functioning icon and theme toggles, paper rendering, no page errors, and no vertical overflow at 1366 x 768. Visually inspected primary, alternate and paper screenshots. Dedicated server on port 8685 leaves the Rover review server untouched.

`households/DESIGN.md` records the main constraint: PW6 cumulative evidence cannot be promised by the old single-icon paper format. Households needs a bounded context encoding, exact paper-derived polls and coverage tests at 15 and 200 before event printouts. Six colors are not an unlimited history encoding. No survival-rate or gameplay validation is claimed yet.

Next: select icon direction, prototype cumulative context lookup and intervention semantics, then a Hard screen using the existing four-team structure. Validate both paper fidelity and complete sessions before integration.
